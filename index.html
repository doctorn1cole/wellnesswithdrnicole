Membership Guidelines

What is this membership for?

A collaborative health partnership, a space to work on root causes, flexible telemedicine, and a consistent touchpoint with a doctor you trust.

What is this membership NOT for?

This is NOT primary care, urgent care, insurance-based, or a replacement for emergency medical services.

How does messaging work?

Messages are for brief questions I can answer in 2-3 minutes. Anything more in-depth will be addressed in our next visit. Essential Care: up to 2 replies/month. Intensive Support: up to 5 replies/month.

Why only 10 members?

This program is starting intentionally small so every member receives thoughtful, personalized, high-quality care that I can be proud of.
